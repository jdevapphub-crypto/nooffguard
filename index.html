About NoOff Guard

Device protection & recovery, built for peace of mind.

What is NoOff Guard?

NoOff Guard is a device protection application designed to help you safeguard your phone and recover it in the event of loss or theft. Once protection is enabled, critical actions such as powering off, disabling GPS, turning off mobile data, or uninstalling the app require PIN verification.

Activation time

Once your payment is confirmed, activation of your NoOff Guard tracker begins immediately. In most cases the tracker is fully enabled in under 1 minute. In rare cases — for example, when the device is offline at the moment of purchase, or when Firebase needs to sync across regions — activation may take up to a maximum of 7 hours.

You can track activation progress from your app: once the "Activate Protection" screen disappears and the dashboard loads, your tracker is live. If more than 7 hours have passed and activation has not completed, please sign out and sign back in — this forces a fresh sync with our servers.

How the tracking portal works

When you sign up inside the NoOff Guard mobile app, a unique device code is generated (for example, NOFF-372030). This code is linked to your device's real-time location, securely transmitted to our servers approximately every 15 seconds while the device is online.

By entering your device code on this portal, you can view the last known location of your device on a live map — and, when using a phone, follow proximity beeps to narrow down its location on the ground.

Additional recovery tools include:

  • Ring Device Now — forces the target phone to play a loud alarm, even when muted.
  • Capture Photo — silently takes a front-camera photo on the target phone and uploads it to your account.
  • Offline detection — alerts you the moment the device reconnects after being offline.

Data & privacy

We only store the information necessary to provide the service: your email, device code, and last known coordinates. Location data is only accessible to someone who knows your unique device code. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: email address and a PIN you provide when registering.
  • Device code: a unique identifier (e.g. NOFF-372030).
  • Location data: approximate GPS coordinates of your registered device, collected at intervals while protection is active.
  • Recovery photos: a front-camera image, only captured when you explicitly trigger the "Capture Photo" action from your account.
  • Searcher location: when you use the proximity radar, your current position is used locally in your browser to compute distance. It is never stored or transmitted by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display the location of your device on this portal when you enter your device code.
  • To verify your identity when disabling protection features.
  • To help recover your device through alarms, photos, and location history.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Who Can See Your Location and Photos

Your location and recovery photos are linked to your device code. Anyone who knows the exact code can view them on this portal. Keep your device code private.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored securely in Google Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Storage. Access is restricted so that only authenticated users can modify their own records. All transmissions use HTTPS.

5. Data Retention

Location data is continuously overwritten with the most recent known position. Recovery photos are retained until you delete them from your account. Account data is retained until you request deletion.

6. Children's Privacy

NoOff Guard is not intended for use by children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at helpnooffguard@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the service means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at helpnooffguard@gmail.com.
